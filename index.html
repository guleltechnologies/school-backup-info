School Backup

Encrypted backups for school application recovery.

What this tool does

School Backup is a private utility operated by Gulel Technologies. It is designed to encrypt school application database and file backups before storing them in an operator-controlled Google Drive account, and to restore them when needed.

Authorized operators can also copy a backup to a separate encrypted repository on their own computer. This information website does not upload, download or display backup contents.

Google account access

Only the designated backup account owner authorizes this integration. Students, parents and school staff do not need to connect a Google account.

The integration requests drive.file permission to create, read, update and delete backup repository files created through this application. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Photos, contacts or unrelated Drive documents.

Privacy and support

Read our privacy policy for details about access, storage, retention and revoking authorization.

For support or a backup-data deletion request, contact Gulel Technologies through the user support email displayed on the Google authorization screen for this application.
